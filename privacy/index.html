Privacy Policy

The Reel Archive Uploader · Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy describes how The Reel Archive Uploader (“the app”), a private tool used by the operator of the YouTube channel The Reel Archive (@TheReelArchiveTV), handles data. The app is used only by the channel operator to manage their own channel; it is not offered to the public and has no other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • An OAuth authorization token for the channel operator's own Google account, granted once by the operator.
  • With that token the app uploads videos approved by the operator to the operator's channel (scope youtube.upload), reads basic information about that channel and its videos (youtube.readonly) and reads that channel's statistics (yt-analytics.readonly).
  • The app does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or any other YouTube users.

How the data is used and stored

  • The token and the channel statistics are used only to publish the channel's own videos and to report their performance to the operator.
  • They are stored on a private server controlled by the operator, protected by access control, and are never sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties.
  • Channel statistics are kept for at most 30 days in raw form, then only as aggregated numbers.
  • The app does not use cookies and does not track visitors of this website.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (Third-party apps with account access). After revocation, or on request, the operator deletes the stored token and statistics within 7 days.
